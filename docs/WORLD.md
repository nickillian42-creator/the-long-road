# World Route & Settlement Framework — THE LONG ROAD
World bible v1 locked by Nic (2026-10-05). Audit passed against frozen campaign canon. Revisit only with Nic's approval.

## Core world philosophy
The journey should feel like a long post-apocalyptic road narrative: different settlements, cultures, survivors, moral problems, relationships, and consequences along the route. Do NOT copy any existing franchise, characters, settlements, plots, or terminology.

There are no zombies and no supernatural monsters. Danger comes from people, scarcity, weather, isolation, deteriorating infrastructure, conflicting societies, old technology, and the unresolved CUSTODIAN mystery.

Sixty years have passed since the Collapse. America must NOT look like civilization ended yesterday. Multiple generations have lived and rebuilt; different communities have developed different solutions for survival.

A normal playthrough should NOT encounter every settlement or NPC.

## Major route locations

### 1. MERCY, TEXAS — Mile 0 — Population ~186
Home. Council-led community with patched water/power infrastructure, agriculture, clinic, and workshops. Establishes the baseline against which every later society is judged. (See `docs/PROLOGUE.md`, `docs/CHARACTERS.md`.)

### 2. THE CROSSROADS — ~Mile 70 — Population ~40
Former truck stop converted into a traveler/trading community. Old semis form parts of its perimeter. First major source of information about the outside world. Potential early Claire/Daniel breadcrumb: someone remembers another Mercer passing through.

**After the Crossroads: first route split (3A / 3B).**

### 3A. REDWATER — ~Mile 150 — Population ~230
Controls a functioning underground reservoir. Healthy crops, clean water, strong defenses. Redwater refuses to freely supply outsiders — because doing so during an earlier drought nearly destroyed the community. Their policy must be morally complicated, never villainous.

### 3B. HAVEN — ~Mile 175 — Population ~310
Apparently beautiful agricultural community: food, families, music, security. Haven believes individual freedom helped destroy the old world. Everyone contributes and receives food/medicine/protection — but residents are not permitted to simply leave. **Do NOT turn this into a predictable cannibal/cult twist.** The ideological conflict itself is the problem.

### 4. THE TOLLWAY — ~Mile 250 — Population ~90
A road-maintenance society controlling roughly 40 miles of usable highway. They clear wrecks, maintain bridges, mark routes, and suppress banditry — then charge travelers for passage. The player may pay, negotiate, assist them, bypass them, or make an enemy. Their people remember the choice later. **Never written as "evil people charging for a road":** maintaining infrastructure costs labor and lives; some travelers call the Tollkeepers extortionists, others call them the only reason commerce still exists.

### 5. BLACKRIDGE — ~Mile 330 — Population apparently 0
An abandoned settlement, vacated suddenly: doors unlocked, possessions left behind, no obvious massacre. A generator or other infrastructure is inexplicably still operating. First strong indication that active CUSTODIAN-connected infrastructure remains in the world. **Keep explanations withheld.**

**The major I-40 decision occurs around this portion of the campaign.**

### 6A. INTERSTATE 40 / C-07 — dangerous route
Military checkpoints, deliberately blocked traffic, strange emergency routing, abandoned infrastructure, systems that occasionally still function. Potential access to C-07 and major discoveries about 2029/CUSTODIAN. High information reward, high danger. **Do NOT reveal the ultimate truth.**

### 6B. THE FREE ROADS — longer route
Players who obey the warning receive DIFFERENT content, not inferior content: small communities, travelers, convoys, environmental obstacles, and unique NPC stories unavailable on I-40.

### 7. LAST STOP — ~Mile 450 — Population ~120
Community built through an old rail yard: rail cars become homes, shops, clinic, bar/lodging. Lively human-story location. Someone here encountered Claire/Daniel relatively recently — escalating the spouse trail.

### 8. THE REPUBLIC OF MORROW — ~Mile 520 — Population ~600
Largest society encountered. Elections, courts, guards, schools, local currency, limited electricity. Genuine political disagreements over expansion, isolation, and alliances. **Do not make one political side objectively correct.** The player's knowledge of Colorado/CUSTODIAN may become politically valuable.

### 9. HOLLOW CREEK — ~Mile 620 — Population ~75
Colorado mountain settlement. Small, interdependent community. Major Josh storyline location: someone recognizes Josh from the 11→4 journey ~20 years ago — his group passed through, and someone there remembers what happened and what Josh did. Guilt, not malice. (Retcon 2026-10-05: replaces the continuity-network connection.)

### 10. THE RELAY — ~Mile 690
Pre-Collapse communications installation, partially functional. Evidence proves Claire/Daniel was here recently. This transitions the personal quest into **THEY'RE CLOSE**.

### 11. CONTINUITY STATION SEVEN — ~Mile 780
Destination and substantial final-act environment. Power, clean water, medical infrastructure, sealed and recently used areas. The Claire/Daniel reunion occurs around this stage; the spouse joins the party. Roughly 4–5 hours of post-reunion spouse-active gameplay across the Station Seven endgame (see runtime clarification in `docs/CAMPAIGN_MASTER_PLAN.md`).

### 12. THE CORE
Deep Station Seven / CUSTODIAN environment and final decision space. Its nature is defined as writer canon in `docs/MYSTERIES.md`; it remains unresolved for the player until the final act.

## Route splits
- **Split 1 (after Crossroads, ~mile 70+):** REDWATER (3A) vs. HAVEN (3B). Rejoin at the Tollway (~250).
- **Split 2 (around Blackridge, ~mile 330):** INTERSTATE 40 / C-07 (6A, dangerous, high-information) vs. THE FREE ROADS (6B, longer, different content). Rejoin at Last Stop (~450).

## Recurring factions
Mercy · Redwater Authority · Haven · Tollkeepers · Republic of Morrow · Free Caravans · CUSTODIAN-related systems/infrastructure.

**CUSTODIAN is NOT a conventional human faction**, and its intentions remain ambiguous. Smaller factions may emerge naturally during NPC/campaign design.

*Note: "Free Caravans" (faction) vs. "Free Roads" (route 6B) — the caravans are the people; the Free Roads are the path. Clarify in writing that the faction is encountered primarily along 6B but exists independently of the route choice.*

## Micro-settlement principle
The world also holds small communities between major locations: an occupied fire station, a roadside motel run by a couple, a radio-tower family, a highway-interchange community, a school converted into housing, an isolated trading post, etc. These examples establish scale and direction — **they are not all mandatory canon yet.** Some locations may hold only a handful of NPCs and one memorable story.

## Route design principle (locked)
Route choices create **genuinely different experiences, never Good Route / Bad Route.** A typical first playthrough sees approximately **7–9 major communities/locations**, plus micro-settlements and road encounters. Players should compare playthroughs and discover different people, settlements, evidence, and consequences. **Missing content is acceptable and desirable.** No route contains all important lore — but every route contains enough information to understand the main story.

## NPC design consequence (locked)
The ~100 authored NPC target is populated **through these locations**, not as 100 disconnected characters. NPCs may remain local, travel, disappear, die, join other settlements, recur hundreds of miles later, become allies or enemies, affect later encounters, and remember how the player treated them. A normal run encounters ~30–45 of the ~100. **No generic "survivor #4" design** — even small NPCs get a name, a motivation, and a reason for being where they are.

## Spouse-trail pacing (locked)
- Crossroads (~70): vague memory — *"another Mercer."* (FIND THEM)
- Last Stop (~450): someone encountered Claire/Daniel relatively recently. (FOLLOW THE TRAIL)
- The Relay (~690): proof of recent presence → **THEY'RE CLOSE**
- Station Seven (~780): reunion → **FOUND** → **GET HOME TOGETHER**
- *Audit note: consider an optional mid-trail breadcrumb around Morrow (~520) — e.g., a trader carrying the spouse's object — to bridge the 450→690 gap.*

## Integration notes with existing game content (Dwight audit, 2026-10-05)
- **Stranger encounter (~mile 140)** sits just before the Redwater/Haven split. No conflict; the C-07 tag remains a hint, not yet infrastructure.
- **Custodian sentry / THE WATCHER (~mile 330)** coincides with Blackridge. Recommendation: fold the sentry into the Blackridge approach with a clear revelation escalation — C-07 tag (hint) → Blackridge's still-running generator (first *active* infrastructure) → sentry (active system demanding authorization). This preserves Blackridge's "first strong indication" framing.
- **Mountain passage (~mile 590)** flows into the Hollow Creek region (~620). Josh's 11→4 history surfaces here — he is driving back toward where it happened.
- **Region system** (North Texas 0–190 / Panhandle 190–390 / Dead Corridor 390–580 / Mountain Passage 580–780) accommodates all locations without breaking existing quest objectives.
- **Lore catalog assignment** (which of the 20 lore items lives on 6A vs. 6B) is future work; both branches must independently carry enough for the main story.
- **7–9 location target:** a completionist run currently counts ~10–11 stops. The Tollway bypass option already supports skipping; ensure at least 2–3 majors remain skippable so the target is reachable.
